## Is there a Navicat for PostgreSQL discount code?

I found no promo code on Navicat's store pages, and nothing on the product page implies you need one. The price is applied directly. Discounts on this product come from three places, and none of them involve typing a code into a box.

The first is the billing term. A perpetual license is a one-time payment that includes a year of software maintenance: upgrades, live chat, technical support and key resets during that year. A subscription keeps you on the current version for as long as you keep paying, which costs less up front and more over time if you stay.

The second is the edition. Enterprise and Non-Commercial both include the Data Modeling tool and the BI features. Standard does not. That's the whole functional difference between Standard and Enterprise, according to Navicat's own edition notes, and it's the difference most buyers never check before ordering.

The third is volume. Navicat discounts perpetual licenses at 5 to 9 units (15% off) and 10 or more (20% off). If you're buying for a team, this is the only real discount available on the product, so it's worth ordering together rather than letting everyone buy singly.

## Which edition fits your work

If you connect to a single PostgreSQL database for paid work and have never needed a schema diagram, buy Standard. The Data Modeling tool and the BI features are the parts Enterprise charges for, and a solo developer maintaining one database rarely opens either.

Enterprise earns its keep when the database is a shared artifact. Schema diagrams that go into code review, or dashboards built against the same database without standing up a second tool, are the two use cases. If neither is on your roadmap, you're buying features you'll look at once and close.

Non-Commercial carries Enterprise features with a restriction on use, and Navicat asks you to confirm you meet its requirements before purchase. Students, teachers and non-profit staff generally fit that box. Freelancers billing clients do not. A license bought at the non-commercial rate and then used for client work is the kind of saving that turns into an awkward conversation, and Standard is the safer purchase if there's any chance the line blurs.

Short contracts point to a subscription. If the engagement ends in four months, a monthly or yearly term gets you the current version with nothing to renew afterwards, and it avoids paying for a perpetual license you'll use twice.

Teams of five or more should order in one transaction. The 15% and 20% breakpoints are the only discount on this product, and they only trigger on perpetual licenses bought together.

Two groups should skip this purchase. If you run a handful of queries a week, pgAdmin, DBeaver and psql cost nothing and cover that workload. ## Where it costs more than the sticker price

Let maintenance lapse and your copy keeps working. What stops is everything else: new releases, live chat, technical support and key resets. On a server that gets a major PostgreSQL upgrade every couple of years, that's the point where the maintenance line comes back into the budget, and it's cheaper to renew yearly than to buy a fresh license.

Licensing is per user, not per machine. Four developers need four licenses even if they share two laptops, which is why team purchases should be planned before anyone installs anything.

The key is cross-platform, and you choose Windows, macOS or Linux when you order. Navicat documents moving a key between platforms, so a later switch isn't fatal, but decide before checkout rather than after.

Currency is the last thing to watch. The USD figures above come from Navicat's own store. A reseller page may quote in another currency, so compare the converted total rather than the headline number, especially if your card charges a foreign transaction fee.

## Common questions

**Does one license work on both Windows and Mac?**
One key covers Windows, macOS and Linux, and you choose the platform at purchase. Switching later is documented by Navicat, but pick correctly the first time.

**Can two people share a single license?**
No. Licensing is per user. Two developers, two licenses.

**What happens when the included year of maintenance ends?**
The application keeps running. Upgrades, live chat, technical support and key resets require an active maintenance plan.

**Is there a student discount?**
There's a Non-Commercial edition, not an automatic student price. You confirm eligibility before buying, and the license is restricted to non-commercial use.

**Do I need a promo code at checkout?**
No code exists for this product as far as I can tell. The edition, term and quantity you select determine the price.
